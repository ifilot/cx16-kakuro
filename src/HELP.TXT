# Welcome to Kakuro
Kakuro combines a crossword-shaped grid with number sums. Fill each cream cell with a digit from 1 to 9. The dark clue cells tell you which totals to make.

# Reading the clues
The clue above a column gives the sum of the cells below it. The clue to the left of a row gives the sum of the cells to its right. Follow each run until you reach another dark cell or the edge of the grid.

# The two rules
- The digits in a run must add up to its clue.
- A digit may appear only once within that run.

For example, a two-cell run totaling 10 can contain 1 and 9, 2 and 8, 3 and 7, or 4 and 6. It cannot contain 5 and 5. Different runs may use the same combination.

# Writing and erasing
Move the mouse over a cream cell to select it. Press 1-9 to enter a digit. Type another digit to replace it. Backspace or 0 clears the selected cell.

Rose cells contain given numbers. They are part of the puzzle and cannot be changed. Dark cells contain clues or separate the runs; you cannot write in them.

# Checking your entries
Click CHECK to turn verification on or off. Verification compares your entries with the stored solution. Correct entries receive a small check; incorrect entries are shown in rose with a cross.

You can continue editing while checking is on. Turn it off whenever you prefer to work without feedback.

# Finishing a puzzle
When every writing cell matches the solution, the game shows your elapsed time. Press Enter to return to the journal. The puzzle receives a solved mark.

# Returning to the journal
Press Escape. Choose Y to leave or N to continue. The timer continues while the confirmation is open.

Completed puzzles can be played again. An opened mark records that you visited a puzzle; it does not save unfinished entries.

# Choosing a puzzle
The journal contains 96 puzzles across four pages, with 24 cards per page. Boards range from 6x6 to 10x10. Each card shows its number and size.

Filled sakura blossoms show the difficulty rating. Outlined blossoms complete the five-slot display. More filled blossoms indicate a harder puzzle.

Move the mouse over a card to see its details. Click to play, or use the arrow keys and Enter. The page arrows move through the journal.

# Music and information
Open OPTIONS in the journal to turn the music on or off. Sound effects remain enabled when music is off. HELP opens this guide. ABOUT contains project information and credits.

# A useful first step
Look for short runs and runs with only one empty cell. A two-cell clue of 3 must use 1 and 2; a two-cell clue of 17 must use 8 and 9. Use crossing runs to decide the order.

# Reading these pages
Up and Down scroll one line. Page Up and Page Down move one screen. Home returns to the beginning; End goes to the last screen. You can also click the Up and Down controls, use the mouse wheel, or click the scrollbar to move by a screen.

Press Escape to return to puzzle selection.
